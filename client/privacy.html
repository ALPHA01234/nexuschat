Privacy Policy

Policy version: 2026-09-04 · Starter policy for pre-launch use

Important: This starter policy must be reviewed and completed with the operator’s legal identity, contact details, hosting providers, retention periods, and applicable-law disclosures before a public/commercial launch.

1. Information NexusChat processes

  • Account information such as username, display name, email address, password hash, verification status, profile picture, banner, bio, status, and preferences.
  • Social and communication information such as friend relationships, messages, message metadata, attachments, call records needed to operate the feature, blocks, and reports or moderation warnings.
  • Technical information such as IP-derived request data, timestamps, browser/network information made available to the service, authentication/session data, and security logs.
  • Plan information such as Premium status, Nexus Coins balance, and feature entitlements.

2. Why the information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, deliver messages and calls, send verification/password-reset codes, provide requested features, prevent abuse, enforce rules, troubleshoot the service, and administer Premium or virtual-credit features.

3. Passwords and verification codes

Passwords should be stored as one-way hashes rather than readable passwords. OTP verification codes are stored in hashed form with short expiration periods. Never share an OTP with another person.

4. Service providers

NexusChat may rely on infrastructure providers for hosting, databases, email delivery, storage, analytics, security, or future advertising. Those providers process data only as needed for the services configured by the operator. The final public policy should list material providers where required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

Account and communication data is retained while needed to provide the service and for legitimate security, moderation, backup, or legal purposes. Users may request or use available account deletion controls. Some records may need to remain for a limited period where legally or operationally necessary.

6. Security

NexusChat uses technical safeguards such as authenticated API access, password hashing, rate limiting, and restricted administrator functions. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

NexusChat is not intended for children below the minimum age stated in the Terms. If the operator learns that an ineligible child’s data was collected, the account should be removed as required by applicable law.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or obtain a copy of certain personal data. The final public policy should provide an official method to submit privacy requests.

9. Policy changes

Material changes should be communicated in the service. NexusChat may record which policy version a user accepted and when.
