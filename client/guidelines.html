NEXUSCHAT

Community Guidelines

Policy version: 2026-09-04

NexusChat is built for conversation, friendship, and communities. These rules apply to profiles, messages, files, calls, and other service activity.

Be respectful

Do not harass, bully, stalk, threaten, or repeatedly target another person. Hate-based attacks and encouragement of violence are not allowed.

Protect privacy and consent

Do not publish another person’s private identifying information without permission. Do not distribute non-consensual intimate material or secretly captured sexual content.

Protect minors

Any sexual exploitation, grooming, or sexualized content involving minors is prohibited. Apparent child sexual abuse material should be removed and handled in accordance with applicable reporting obligations.

No scams, malware, or manipulation

Do not use NexusChat for phishing, fraud, malicious files, credential theft, spam, fake giveaways, deceptive payment schemes, or attempts to compromise another account or device.

Respect intellectual property

Share material only when you own it or have permission to use it. Repeated infringement may lead to account action.

No impersonation

Do not misleadingly impersonate another person, organization, staff member, or verified entity.

Enforcement

Depending on severity and history, NexusChat administrators may issue a warning, restrict features, suspend access, remove content, or ban an account. Serious safety or legal issues may be escalated where required.
